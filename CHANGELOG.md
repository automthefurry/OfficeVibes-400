# Changes

## 0.9 — the OfficeVision calendar

- The calendar now opens on a **Process Calendars** menu, with day, N-day, business-week, boxed month and six-month views, printing, conference rooms, meetings with clash checking, shared calendars, holidays and calendar search.
- The main menu is shorter, with submenus for mail and chat, the organizer, documents, and games.
- The build reports screen files that are in use, instead of failing with no reason given.
- Fixes for the reminder service, the calendar screen file and Tetris.

## 0.8

- Calendar: change appointments, appointments over several days, and all-day separate from midnight.
- The reminder service, and a pop-up messages setting in My profile.
- Tetris, and a tic-tac-toe opponent that cannot be beaten.
- Spreadsheets: type straight into the cells, Tab between them, Enter moves down or right, highlighted row and column, and bar charts.

## 0.7

- Every application has its own main screen: message center, mailbox, notepad, to-do list with a progress bar, phone book with cards, document cards and more.
- Live chat compiles.

## 0.6

- Spreadsheets with formulas.

## 0.5

- Renamed OfficeVibes/400. Live chat. A choice of animated characters on the calendar.

## 0.4

- The look of the original screens, search across everything, and help on every screen.

## 0.3

- The full suite in RPG IV: users, administration, logging, lookups, calendar, documents, printing, games and the office cat.

## 0.2

- The earlier C version, ported to RPG IV.
