# User guide

Start OfficeVibes/400 with `GO OFFICE/OFFICE`, or sign on if it has been made your first screen.

## Things that work everywhere

- **Pick with the cursor.** On any menu or list, put the cursor on a line and press Enter instead of typing a number or an option.
- **F1 or Help** shows a help page for the screen you are on.
- **F3** leaves; **F12** goes back one screen.
- **F4 on a "To" or user field** opens a window of enrolled users. Type part of a name in Find to narrow it.
- **F7 and F8** page backward and forward through long lists.
- **Print keys** write a spooled report. Find it under **Printed output** on the main menu.

## The main menu

| Option | Opens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Calendar |
| 2 | Mail, messages and chat (a menu) |
| 3 | Notes, tasks and phone book (a menu) |
| 4 | Documents, spreadsheets and printed output (a menu) |
| 5 | Search everything |
| 6 | Office directory: everyone, with department and phone |
| 7 | Games and the office cat (a menu) |
| 8 | Printed output |
| 9 | Settings |
| 10 | Administration (administrators only) |
| 90 | Sign off the menu |

The counts beside the options show appointments today, new mail and messages, and open tasks.

## Calendar

The calendar opens on **Process Calendars**. Type a number and press Enter:

| Option | Does |
|---|---|
| 1 | Work with the schedule for the chosen day |
| 2 | View several days (type how many, up to 31) |
| 3 | Conference rooms and what is booked in them |
| 4, 5 | The next day, the day before |
| 6 | The month, as boxes |
| 7 | Schedule a meeting |
| 8 | Print several days |
| 9 | The business week, Monday to Friday by the hour |
| 10 | Six months at a glance |
| 11 | Who may look at or change your calendar |
| 12 | Search the calendar |
| 13 | Company holidays |

- **Calendar for**: type another user's ID to open their calendar, if they allow it. Room calendars start with `#`, such as `#ROOM1`.
- **Date**: type a date such as `3/15` or `3/15/2027`, or put the cursor on a day in the small calendar and press Enter.
- **F10 and F11** move a month at a time. **F6** adds an appointment. **F5** changes the character beside the calendar.

**Appointments.** Leave the time blank for something that lasts all day; `00:00` means midnight. Fill in **Last day** to repeat an appointment over several days, up to 31. Each day is then marked with its number, such as `(2/5)`.

**In the day schedule**, `2` changes an appointment and `4` deletes it. F7 and F8 go to the previous and next day.

**In the month**, each box has a line of dots for the hours from 8 to 4. A letter in place of a dot is the first letter of an appointment in that hour. `<` means something before 8, `>` something after 4, `*` an all-day appointment, and `Hol` a company holiday.

**Meetings.** Give a subject, date, time, up to six people and, if you like, a room. Clashes in the same hour are listed in red; F10 books the meeting anyway. It goes on every calendar, and each person gets a message.

## Messages

Short, one-line messages. **F6** sends one, **F9** sends to everyone, and **F10** switches between your inbox and what you sent. Option `5` shows a message in the box at the bottom, `R` replies and `4` deletes. You can address a user, a distribution group, or `ALL`.

## Mail

Memos with a subject and up to eight lines. **F6** writes one, and option `5` opens one. While reading, **F6** replies, **F9** forwards and **F10** prints.

## Live chat

Chat opens on a list of **group rooms**, newest topic first, so you can see where people are talking. Every room is open to everyone in the office, and `EVERYONE` is always there.

- `1`, or the cursor on a room, joins it. **F6** makes a new room: a one-word name and a topic.
- Inside a group room, **F9** changes the topic. Everyone in the room sees a line saying so, and the room moves to the top of the list.
- `4` deletes a room you made. Administrators can delete any room except EVERYONE.
- For a private chat, type a user ID in **Private chat with**, or press F4 to pick someone. The first time you write, they get a message saying you are waiting.

The latest 14 lines are shown, and new lines appear by themselves. Type a line and press Enter to send it. **F6** goes back to the room list.

## Notes, tasks and phone book

- **Notes**: private notes on a yellow pad. F6 adds a note; `2` changes and `4` deletes. Find narrows the list.
- **Tasks**: a to-do list with a progress bar. Each task can have a due date and a priority (H, M or L). Finished tasks are blue and overdue ones red. `2` marks a task done, `9` reopens it and `4` deletes it. **F11** clears every finished task.
- **Phone book**: your own contacts. Put the cursor on a letter of the A to Z line and press Enter to jump to it. `5` shows a contact card, `2` changes an entry and `4` deletes it.

## Documents

Documents are listed one to a line, fourteen to a page, with title, size and date changed. `2` or the cursor opens the editor, `6` prints the document and `4` deletes it.

In the editor you type on fifteen lines at a time. **F7 and F8** move through the document, **F6** inserts a line at the cursor, **F9** deletes the line at the cursor, **F10** saves and **F11** prints. F3 warns you once if there are unsaved changes.

## Spreadsheets

Each sheet has columns A to H and rows 1 to 99. Type straight into the cells.

- **Tab and Backtab** move across, and the arrow keys work too. **Field Exit** clears the rest of a cell and moves on, the quick way to type over a number.
- **Enter** stores what you typed and moves down one cell, or right after **F10**.
- A cell holds text, a number or a **formula** starting with `=`: `=B2*C2+10`, `=SUM(B2:B9)`, `=AVG(...)`, `=MIN(...)`, `=MAX(...)`, `=COUNT(...)`. Multiplying and dividing come before adding and subtracting; there are no brackets.
- The line below the grid shows the current cell's formula. Use **Contents** for anything longer than eight characters.
- **F6** empties the cell, **F9** prints and **F11** draws a bar chart of the current column.

## Search everything

Type a word and press Enter. Search looks through your messages, mail, calendar, notes, tasks, phone book and documents, and the office directory. `5` or the cursor shows the whole item.

## Games and the cat

- **Guess the number**: 1 to 100, in as few tries as you can.
- **Tic-tac-toe**: the computer plays perfectly, so a draw is the best you can do.
- **Tetris**: F4 left, F5 right, Enter or F6 turn, F7 down one, F8 drop and F9 new game. Your best score is kept.
- **The office cat**: F5 pets it, F6 feeds it and F7 plays with it. It gets hungry while you are away.

## Settings

Your name, department and phone number, as others see them. **Pop-up messages**: Y makes reminders, mail notices and chat requests appear the moment they arrive; N lets them wait in your message queue.

- **Animations**: N turns off everything that moves by itself (the calendar screen saver, and chat refreshing on its own). Tetris still falls.
- **Character**: COFFEE, CAT, DOG, ROBOT, FISH or NONE, shown on the calendar menu.
- **Screen saver after**: how many minutes the calendar waits, with no keys pressed, before its screen saver starts. 0 means never. Any key brings the calendar back.
- **Chat refresh**: A shows new lines as they arrive; M only when you press F5.

**Calendar and clock**

- **Time zone**: your offset from UTC, such as `-05:00` or `+01:00`. Blank means you work in office time. New appointments use your zone and are stored in office time, so everyone sees the same thing. The day screen shows the time now in the office and in your zone.
- **Week starts on**: `S` (Sunday) or `M` (Monday), for every month on the screen.
- **Working day**: such as 08:00 to 17:00. New appointments start at the start of your day, and the business week and month views are drawn around these hours.
- **Units**: `I` for a 12-hour clock and month/day/year; `M` for metric: a 24-hour clock and day.month.year. Dates typed with dots, such as `15.3.2027`, are always read day first.

Nothing redraws on a timer while you are typing in a field any more, so the cursor stays where you put it.
